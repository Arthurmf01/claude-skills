# Sender rules (example)

Copy this file to `rules.md` in the installed skill folder and replace everything with your own. The critic reads it before judging and applies it on top of its general bar. The sender here is invented.

## Who is sending

Sam Okafor. Six years in enterprise software sales, finishing an MBA in December, moving into applied AI.

## Cold messages

Shape, in this order:

1. A hook specific to the recipient.
2. One line of track record: "grew a product line from $0 to $8M ARR across 12 markets".
3. The transition, stated plainly: finishing the MBA in December.
4. One ask.
5. The offer: to share what I am learning building agents alongside the degree.

## Warm messages

Open with the shared context (where we met, the last thing we talked about). Leave the track-record line out: they know it. Then the ask.

## Phrases I do not use

- "pick your brain"
- "I hope this finds you well"
- "synergy"

## House style

- British English spelling.
- No em-dashes. Use a comma, a colon, or a new sentence.
- No exclamation marks.
- Sign off with first name only.
